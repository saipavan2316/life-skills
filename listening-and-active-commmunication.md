# Listening and Active Communication

## 1. Active Listening

#### Question 1 - What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

### Answer -

1. **Give full attention** - Focus on the speaker without getting distracted by your phone or other activities.
2. **Maintain eye contact** - Show the speaker that you are interested in the conversation.
3. **Avoid interrupting** - Allow the speaker to complete their thoughts before responding.
4. **Show interest** - Use gestures like nodding to show that you are following the conversation.
5. **Ask questions** - Ask relevant questions whenever something is unclear.
6. **Paraphrase** - Repeat the main points in your own words to confirm your understanding.
7. **Observe body language** - Pay attention to facial expressions and gestures.
8. **Provide feedback** - Respond appropriately to show that you understand the speaker.

## 2. Reflective Listening

#### Question 2 - According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

### Answer -

1. **Listen carefully** - Give complete attention to the speaker and understand what they are trying to communicate.
2. **Observe emotions** - Understand the feelings and emotions behind the speaker's words.
3. **Reflect the message** - Repeat or rephrase the speaker's message in your own words.
4. **Clarify doubts** - Ask questions to make sure that you have understood the message correctly.
5. **Confirm understanding** - Verify with the speaker whether your understanding is correct.
6. **Avoid assumptions** - Do not judge or make conclusions without understanding the complete message.

Reflective listening helps avoid misunderstandings and makes communication more effective.

## 3. Reflection

#### Question 3 - What are the obstacles in your listening process?

### Answer -

1. Sometimes I get distracted by my thoughts while someone is speaking.
2. I occasionally start thinking about my response before the speaker finishes.
3. Background noise and other distractions affect my concentration.
4. Sometimes I assume that I already understand the topic without listening completely.
5. Technical discussions with too many details can be difficult to remember.
6. Lack of familiarity with certain terms can make it difficult to follow conversations.

#### Question 4 - What can you do to improve your listening?

### Answer -

1. Avoid distractions and focus completely on the speaker.
2. Listen patiently without interrupting.
3. Take notes during meetings and technical discussions.
4. Ask questions whenever I have doubts instead of making assumptions.
5. Summarize important points to confirm my understanding.
6. Practise listening without immediately thinking about my response.
7. Pay attention to the speaker's tone and body language.

## 4. Types of Communication

#### Question 5 - When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?

### Answer -

I sometimes use passive communication when I am talking to seniors or people with more experience. I hesitate to express my opinions because I worry that my suggestions might be wrong or that others may disagree with me. Sometimes I avoid saying no even when I am uncomfortable with something.

#### Question 6 - When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

### Answer -

Sometimes when I get frustrated or when things do not go as expected, I may respond in a harsh tone. This can happen when someone repeatedly makes the same mistake or when I feel that my opinion is not being considered. I need to learn to control my emotions and express my concerns calmly.

#### Question 7 - When do you switch into Passive Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

### Answer -

Sometimes when I feel disappointed or irritated with someone, I avoid expressing my feelings directly. Instead, I may become silent, give short replies or use sarcasm to show that I am upset. I understand that this can create misunderstandings, so I should communicate my feelings openly.

#### Question 8 - How can you make your communication assertive? What are a few steps you can apply in your own life?

### Answer -

1. **Express my thoughts clearly** - Share my opinions and ideas confidently without being rude.
2. **Learn to say no** - Politely refuse requests when I cannot take additional responsibilities.
3. **Respect others' opinions** - Listen to different viewpoints even when I disagree with them.
4. **Use clear language** - Explain my needs and concerns directly instead of expecting others to understand them.
5. **Control emotions** - Stay calm during disagreements and avoid reacting immediately out of frustration.
6. **Use I-statements** - Express my feelings using statements like "I feel" or "I think" instead of blaming others.
7. **Ask for clarification** - If I disagree or do not understand something, ask questions instead of making assumptions.
8. **Accept feedback** - Take constructive criticism positively and use it to improve myself.

Assertive communication helps me express my thoughts confidently while respecting other people's feelings and opinions. I want to practise this in my daily life, especially during team discussions and workplace conversations.
