# Learning Process

## Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where we understand a concept by explaining it in simple words as if teaching someone else.

## Question 2: In the Barbara Oakley video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

The most interesting idea was that taking breaks can help our brain solve problems. Switching between focused learning and relaxation helps us understand concepts better and make new connections.

## Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

- **Focused Mode:** Concentrating deeply on a particular topic or problem.
- **Diffuse Mode:** Relaxed thinking that helps the brain make connections and approach problems from different perspectives.

## Question 4: According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

1. Break down the skill into smaller parts.
2. Learn enough to self-correct.
3. Remove barriers to practice.
4. Practise for at least 20 hours.

## Question 5: What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

1. Use the Feynman Technique to understand and explain concepts in my own words.
2. Give 100% attention to my work and avoid distractions.
3. Break complex topics into smaller parts.
4. Practise coding regularly instead of focusing only on theory.
5. Implement every software concept through small programs and projects.
6. Revise concepts and identify gaps in my understanding.
7. Set deadlines for completing assignments and practice exercises.
8. Take breaks when needed to improve my problem-solving ability.
9. Communicate proactively whenever I face difficulties.
10. Focus on understanding concepts deeply rather than just completing tasks.
