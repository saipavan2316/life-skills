# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (PoSH)

## Question 1 - What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

### Answer -

Sexual harassment includes any unwanted behaviour of a sexual nature that makes someone feel uncomfortable, unsafe or disrespected.

Examples include:

- Unwanted physical contact, touching or inappropriate gestures.
- Making sexual jokes, comments or inappropriate remarks.
- Sending unwanted sexual messages, images or videos.
- Making repeated unwanted requests for dates or sexual favours.
- Staring, making inappropriate facial expressions or showing offensive material.
- Asking for sexual favours in exchange for job opportunities, promotions or other benefits.
- Repeatedly making inappropriate comments about someone's appearance or personal life.

Such behaviour can happen in person, online or through workplace communication.

## Question 2 - What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

### Answer -

If I face or witness any form of sexual harassment, I would take the following steps:

1. **Speak up** - If it is safe to do so, clearly communicate that the behaviour is inappropriate and must stop.
2. **Document the incident** - Record the date, time, location and details of what happened. Preserve relevant messages, emails or other evidence.
3. **Report the incident** - Inform the manager, HR or the company's Internal Committee (IC) through the appropriate reporting process.
4. **Support the affected person** - Listen without judging them and encourage them to report the incident if they feel comfortable.
5. **Maintain confidentiality** - Avoid sharing incident details with unrelated people or spreading rumours.
6. **Follow up** - Cooperate with the investigation and ensure the matter is handled through the proper procedure.

I believe everyone deserves a safe and respectful workplace. I would never ignore such behaviour, whether I experience it myself or witness it happening to someone else.
